# JOSIE-Voice

JOSIE-Voice is a voice assistant for your Mac. Talk to her like a person, or type when you can't talk. Listening, voice recognition and speech run on your Mac, and your conversations stay there. No account needed.

**Website:** https://goekdeniz-guelmez.github.io/JOSIE-Voice/

## Download

Get the latest `.dmg` from [Releases](https://github.com/Goekdeniz-Guelmez/JOSIE-Voice/releases), open it and drag JOSIE-Voice into Applications.

Current builds are test builds and are ad-hoc signed. The first time, right-click the app and choose **Open**, or go to **System Settings → Privacy & Security** and click **Open Anyway**.

## Requirements

- A Mac with Apple Silicon (M1 or newer)
- macOS 14 Sonoma or newer
- About 7 GB of free space (models download on first launch)
- 16 GB of memory recommended for the larger models
